FAQ

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

When does My Photo Club launch?

Founding doors open on 17 November, for people on the waitlist first. There are only 100 founding spots.

How much does it cost?

Founding members pay €4.99/month, and that price stays locked in for as long as you remain a member. After the founding round, membership will be €7.99/month, with an annual plan that gives you one month free.

When will I be charged?

Only if you decide to join when the doors open. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime from your member dashboard.

What will I find in the club?

A monthly photo challenge, honest feedback on your photos, practical courses in your member dashboard and behind-the-shot stories. Founding members get the first course and challenge from day one.

How is this different from Instagram?

There's no likes race and no algorithm deciding who sees your work. Your photos are seen by people who care about photography, and the feedback is about the craft, not the numbers.

Who is the club for?

For anyone who loves photography, whether you're just starting out or already more experienced.

Do I need a professional camera?

No. Any camera works, and so does a phone, as long as you take photography seriously.

How do I join the waitlist?

Fill in the form with your first name and email. You'll get an email as soon as the founding doors open.
